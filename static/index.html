FINN

An investor's inbox for an NSE portfolio. It reads every exchange filing and end-of-day move, scores each one for materiality, and shows only what matters.

Push: Today groups each window's filings into events; Alerts fire once when your rules are crossed, fed by the live NSE announcements feed.

Pull: Research builds 8 views of any NSE company from its annual reports, concalls and statements. Ask FINN answers in chat, typed or spoken.
